Lagoon Rally privacy policy

This policy covers the Android game Lagoon Rally, including the closed test on Google Play.

While you play

Lagoon Rally does not collect or share personal data. You can play alone or share one screen with a second player, without creating an account. The game reads your controls and your device’s graphics and thermal information to run the race and adjust graphics quality. It has no advertising, in-app purchases, online multiplayer or chat.

Saved races and settings

The game saves an unfinished race on your device so you can resume. The save includes each car’s position on the virtual course, progress and race time. The game also remembers your camera, sound and player-count settings. These records stay in app storage. The game disables Android cloud backup and sends no race or device information to the developer. You can erase the local save and preferences in Android Settings by opening Apps, choosing Lagoon Rally, opening Storage and selecting Clear data or Clear storage.

Questions

To contact the developer about privacy or a deletion request, use the support email shown under App support on Lagoon Rally’s Google Play listing. If you send feedback outside the game, the recipient can see the contact details and information you choose to send.
